Our Core Beliefs

Rooted in Scripture, Guided by Faith

What We Believe

The foundational truths that anchor our church family in life and faith.

01
We believe the Bible is God’s true and trustworthy Word, guiding us in life and faith.
02
We believe in one God who exists as the Father, the Son (Jesus Christ), and the Holy Spirit.
03
We believe Jesus is God’s Son. He was born of a virgin, lived a perfect life, did miracles, died on the cross for our sins, and rose from the dead. He is alive today and will return in power and glory.
04
We believe everyone needs salvation, and the only way to be saved is through Jesus. His death and resurrection give us new life. We show our commitment to Him through baptism and remember His sacrifice through communion.
05
We believe the Holy Spirit lives in us, helping us grow in faith, make good choices, and live for God.
06
We believe one day, all people will be resurrected—those who follow Jesus will have eternal life, and those who reject Him will be separated from God.
07
We believe all Christians are united in Jesus, called to love God and each other.
