Welcome to New Grace MBC!

We’re so glad you're here! Take a moment to explore and get to know us. Whether you're visiting for the first time or looking for a church to call home, we invite you and your family to join us for one of our weekly services.

At New Grace MBC, we believe in doing life together as a family—growing in faith, supporting one another, and building a strong foundation rooted in Christ. We are committed to living the way we were created to live, loving the way we were created to love, and serving the way we were created to serve.

Have questions? Drop us a message in our online guest book or connect with one of our Pastors or Ministry Leaders—we're here for you! If you need prayer, send us a request online. No matter what you're facing, God is listening, and we are standing with you in faith.

We pray you and your family experience God's presence through worship, fellowship, and community. We can't wait to connect with you. See you soon!

Many blessings,
The New Grace Family
