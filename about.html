About Us

Our Purpose & Vision

Welcome to New Grace Missionary Baptist Church

At New Grace, we are fully dedicated to adding to the body of Christ through intentional faith, rich fellowship, and a commitment to spiritual growth. As a vibrant Baptist church family rooted in Birmingham, AL, our foundation is built squarely upon the Word of God and the transforming power of His grace.

"Live! Love! Serve!" — Our core calling is to live the way we're supposed to live, love the way we're supposed to love, and serve the way we're supposed to serve.

We believe that church is more than just a Sunday gathering—it is a supportive family where you can truly belong, heal, and thrive. Whether you are taking your first steps in faith or looking for a church home to plant deep roots, there is a place for you here.

Our Three Pillars

Live

Living out our faith authentically every day, walking in the light of God’s commands and truth.

Love

Loving one another deeply, reflecting Christ’s unconditional love, compassion, and grace to our community.

Serve

Serving our church, our neighbors, and the world with humble hearts and dedicated hands.

We invite you to join us for worship and experience the fellowship of the New Grace family firsthand. Come grow with us!
